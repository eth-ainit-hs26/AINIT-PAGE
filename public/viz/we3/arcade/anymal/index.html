ANYMAL

Photo: ANYbotics AG

Meet ANYmal, ETH Zurich's quadrupedal robot, in a tiny grid-world game.

HOW TO PLAY

ANYmal moves on a grid trying to collect rewards while two spooky ghosts roam around. Sometimes the robot malfunctions and moves randomly: welcome to the world of stochastic decision-making.
🎮
You drive ANYmal with the arrow buttons under the grid, or with w/a/s/d or the arrow keys: up, left, down, right.
⭐
Collect rewards. A new reward spawns at a random cell after each one you pick up.
👻
Avoid the ghosts. Two ghosts wander the grid following random walks.
⚡
Mind the glitches. ANYmal occasionally malfunctions and moves in a random direction.
🏁
Game over when ANYmal collides with a ghost or the round limit is reached.
🎯
Goal: maximise the cumulative score over a fixed number of rounds.
